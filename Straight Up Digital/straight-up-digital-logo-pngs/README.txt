STRAIGHT UP DIGITAL
PRODUCTION BRAND PACK 1.0

This pack contains individual SVG vector logo files, transparent PNG exports,
favicon files, and a PDF brand guide.

Logo system:
01 to 09: SVG vector assets.
10 to 17: transparent PNG logo exports.
18 to 21: app icon and favicon assets.
22: PDF brand guidelines.

Brand colours:
Obsidian Black #080A09
Electric Lime #A8FF65
Graphite #252B27
Soft Ivory #F2F4EF

Typography direction:
Headings: Montserrat
Body: Inter

Primary tagline:
INTELLIGENCE. VISIBILITY. GROWTH.

Note:
The SVG logo uses editable text with Montserrat and Inter font-family declarations.
For consistent rendering, install those fonts or convert text to outlines in a vector
editor before sending final artwork to a commercial printer. The mark is a clean,
scalable interpretation of the approved metallic S and upward arrow concept.
